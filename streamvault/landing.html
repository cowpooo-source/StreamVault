Install the App

Portal Heaven is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it directly from your browser.

  1. Open Portal Heaven in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner.
  1. Open Portal Heaven in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Menu icon (three dots) in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm.
  1. Open Portal Heaven in Chrome, Edge, or Brave.
  2. Look for the Install icon (a screen with a down arrow) on the right side of the address bar.
  3. Click it and select Install.
