StreamVault Refund Policy

1. 7-Calendar-Day Self-Service Refund Guarantee

StreamVault offers a full 7-calendar-day self-service refund guarantee on all Standard 30-Day Pass purchases and initial recurring subscription payments.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a self-service refund:

  • The refund request must be submitted within 7 calendar days (168 hours) of the original transaction timestamp.
  • The payment must not have been previously disputed or partially refunded.
  • The request is initiated through the authenticated Billing Settings interface on the HTTPS portal.

3. Refund Processing & Entitlement Adjustment

Upon submitting an eligible refund request, the payment status transitions to refund_pending. Once Stripe confirms the full refund via webhook, the order is marked refunded, the original payment method is credited by Stripe (typically within 5-10 business days), and your account access reverts to the Free tier with a 2-connection limit.

4. Manual Assistance

If you encounter any issues requesting an automated refund, submit a support ticket under the Billing & Refund category or email support@portalheaven.stream with your Order ID.
