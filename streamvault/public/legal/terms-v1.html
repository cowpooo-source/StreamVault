2. Subscription Plans & Entitlements

StreamVault offers multiple access tiers:

  • Free Plan: Access with up to two concurrent connections and a single active login session.
  • Standard 30-Day Pass ($3.99 USD): 30 calendar days of uninterrupted access with up to five concurrent connections and three active logins. Does not auto-renew.
